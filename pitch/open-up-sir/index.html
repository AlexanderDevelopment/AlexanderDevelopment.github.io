Hook

A tiny emergency. A fully equipped fire crew. A house that may not survive the rescue.

Players are called to solve simple emergencies, but physical tools, bad communication, overpowered hoses, destructible houses and panic can turn every rescue into a disaster.

The firefighters are often more dangerous than the fire.

Elevator Pitch

A crew that should help. Tools that fight back.

OPEN UP SIR! is a 2-4 player first-person physics comedy about an underprepared firefighting crew in a small Bavarian town. Players receive emergency calls, race to the scene in a shared firetruck, and solve incidents ranging from cats in trees to real house fires.

But every tool is physical and dangerous: hoses knock players down, ladders wobble, gas cylinders explode, walls break, and careless rescuing can cost more than the fire itself.

The goal is not just to finish the call - it is to keep the property alive.

USP

Physics chaos players can hear, feel and destroy.

Active ragdoll physics

Players stumble, collide, get knocked down and recover through physical motion.

Proximity voice chat

Communication becomes spatial, messy and funny when the crew spreads across the house.

Online co-op

2-4 players share the truck, tools, mistakes and mission payout in one live run.

Complete destructibility

Walls, furniture, doors and property become part of the score, not just decoration.

The rescue is a system collision: bodies, voices, tools and buildings all fight back.

Co-op & Voice Chat

The team is split by information and responsibility.

Player 1: Driver

During dispatchDrives the truck, follows voice instructions, avoids traffic and property damage.
On the missionPositions the truck, manages parking angle and keeps escape routes open.

Player 2: Navigator

During dispatchReads the map, calls turns, chooses the fastest route.
On the missionTracks objectives, hazards and the shortest path through the building.

Player 3: Truck Operator

During dispatchPrepares hose, ladder, masks, axe and compartments.
On the missionControls water supply, pressure, hose length and refill timing.

Player 4: Breacher / Rescuer

During dispatchPlans entry tools and rescue gear before arrival.
On the missionOpens doors, carries people or cat, disables hazards and finishes objectives.

Roles are not classes. They emerge from the chaos.

Flexible Partnership Options

Choose the structure that matches your role.

A · Passive investor

  • Funds development and marketing budget.
  • Costs recoup first: 70% investor / 30% developer.
  • After recoup: 85% developer / 15% investor for 3 years.
  • Developer keeps IP and production control.

B · Professional publisher

  • Funds development only, around €35K or negotiated share.
  • Owns marketing execution: Jester, trailer, Steam page, staged beats.
  • Includes Keymailer, Lurkit and games media outreach, including IGN.
  • Marketing is publisher contribution and is not recouped.

C · Hybrid / custom

  • 1-2 passive investors plus a marketing-focused publisher is possible.
  • Budget and upside split proportionally to actual contribution.
  • Publisher counteroffers and alternative terms are welcome.
  • Final percentages depend on negotiation and real contribution.

Deal notes
